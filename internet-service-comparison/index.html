Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the right internet speed?
For 1-2 people doing basic browsing, 25-50 Mbps works well. For a household of 3-4 streaming HD video and working from home, 100-300 Mbps is recommended. For large households with heavy 4K streaming and gaming, consider 500 Mbps to 1 Gbps. Use the bandwidth estimator above for a personalized recommendation.
What does cost per Mbps mean?
Cost per Mbps divides your monthly price by the download speed. It measures the value of a plan regardless of price tier. A $60/month 300 Mbps plan at $0.20/Mbps is better value than a $40/month 100 Mbps plan at $0.40/Mbps, even though it costs more per month.
What is a data cap and does it matter?
A data cap limits monthly usage in GB or TB. Exceeding it may trigger overage fees or throttled speeds. Most households use 300-500 GB per month. Heavy streaming, gaming, and remote work households can use 1 TB or more. If you stream 4K content regularly, look for plans with no data cap.
