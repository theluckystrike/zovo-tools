Frequently Asked Questions

What pace do I need to run a sub-4 hour marathon?▼
To finish a marathon in under 4 hours, you need to maintain a pace faster than 9:09 per mile (5:41 per km). A common strategy is to target 9:00/mi pace, which gives you a 3:56 finish with a small buffer. For a negative split approach, you might run the first half at 9:10/mi and the second half at 8:50/mi.
What is a good marathon time for a beginner?▼
For a first-time marathoner, simply finishing is a great accomplishment. The average marathon finish time is around 4:30 for men and 5:00 for women. A common goal for beginners is to finish under 5 hours, which requires about 11:27 per mile. Focus on completing the distance first, then work on improving your time.
Should I run even splits or negative splits?▼
Most coaches recommend even splits or slight negative splits (second half 1-2 minutes faster). Starting too fast is the most common marathon mistake. Research shows the fastest marathon times tend to have very even pacing throughout the race.
How do I convert min/mile to min/km?▼
Divide your min/mile pace by 1.60934. For example, 8:00/mi = 4:58/km. To go the other direction, multiply min/km by 1.60934. A quick approximation: multiply min/km by 1.6 to get min/mi.
What is the Riegel formula?▼
The Riegel formula (T2 = T1 x (D2/D1)^1.06) predicts race times across distances. Published by Peter Riegel in 1977, it remains one of the most widely used prediction models. It works best when the distance ratio is not extreme and assumes consistent training.
What are the Boston Marathon qualifying times?▼
BQ times range from 3:00:00 (men 18-34) to 5:25:00 (women 80+). Meeting the standard does not guarantee entry. In recent years, runners have needed to beat their qualifying time by 5-6 minutes due to high demand.
How does elevation affect marathon pace?▼
Each 100 feet of elevation gain adds roughly 6-8 seconds per mile. A marathon with 1,000 feet of climbing might add 1-2 minutes to your finish time. Above 5,000 feet altitude, reduced oxygen can slow pace by 3-8%.
