How CD Interest Is Calculated

  • Compound A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt), where P = principal, r = annual rate, n = compounding frequency, t = time in years
  • APY (Annual Percentage Yield) accounts for compounding, while APR (Annual Percentage Rate) does not. APY = (1 + r/n)^n - 1
  • More frequent compounding (daily vs annually) yields slightly higher returns due to interest-on-interest
  • Most CDs charge a penalty for early withdrawal, typically several months of interest

CD Investment Tips

CD laddering is a strategy where you divide your investment across multiple CDs with staggered maturity dates. This provides regular access to funds while earning higher long-term rates. Consider the current rate environment: when rates are rising, shorter terms may be better; when rates are falling, locking in longer terms can be advantageous.
