Every semester I'd see students in my feed asking how to calculate their GPA. The formula isn't complicated, but when you've got 5 classes with different credit hours and your brain is fried from finals, a calculator helps. So I built one.

## What It Does

Add your courses — name, credit hours, and grade. The calculator computes your GPA on a 4.0 scale instantly. You can calculate a single semester or your cumulative GPA by entering your existing GPA and total credits alongside new courses.

It handles plus/minus grading (A+, A, A-, B+, etc.) and lets you add as many courses as you need. There's also a "what if" mode where you can see what grade you'd need in remaining courses to hit a target GPA.

## No Signup, No Data Collection

Your grades are your business. I've seen GPA calculators that make you create an account to "save your grades." That's your academic record sitting on someone else's server. Not great.

This tool runs entirely in your browser. No server, no database, no tracking. Your grades are calculated in JavaScript right on the page and never leave your device. Close the tab and they're gone.

## The Target GPA Feature

The most useful part might be the target GPA calculator. Enter your current cumulative GPA, total credits earned, and your target GPA. It tells you what average grade you need across your remaining credits to reach that target. It's a reality check — sometimes encouraging, sometimes sobering, always useful for planning.
