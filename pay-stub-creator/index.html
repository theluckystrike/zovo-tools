Frequently Asked Questions

How are federal taxes calculated on a pay stub?
Federal income tax uses progressive brackets. Your gross pay is annualized (multiplied by pay periods per year), then the standard deduction and pre-tax deductions are subtracted. The remaining taxable income is taxed at rates from 10% to 37%. The annual tax is then divided by your number of pay periods to get the per-period withholding amount.
What is FICA and how much is deducted?
FICA includes Social Security (6.2% on wages up to $168,600 in 2026) and Medicare (1.45% with no cap, plus 0.9% additional on earnings over $200,000 for single filers). Most employees pay a combined 7.65% in FICA taxes. Your employer matches this amount.
What pay frequencies does this tool support?
This pay stub creator supports Weekly (52 pay periods), Bi-Weekly (26 pay periods), Semi-Monthly (24 pay periods), and Monthly (12 pay periods). The frequency affects tax calculations since the annualized income determines which tax brackets apply.
Is this pay stub legally valid?
This tool generates estimated pay stubs for informational and planning purposes. While the calculations use current tax rates and formulas, actual payroll involves additional factors like W-4 allowances, local taxes, and employer-specific policies. For official payroll, use a certified payroll service. This tool is useful for budgeting, understanding deductions, and creating pay stub templates.
