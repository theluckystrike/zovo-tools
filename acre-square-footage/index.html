Frequently Asked Questions

How many square feet are in one acre?

One acre equals exactly 43,560 square feet. This is the standard measurement used in the United States for land area. An acre is also equivalent to 4,840 square yards, roughly 4,047 square meters, or about 0.4047 hectares. If you picture a square plot that is one acre, each side would measure approximately 208.71 feet.

How big is an acre compared to a football field?

A standard American football field including both end zones measures 360 feet by 160 feet, giving it a total area of 57,600 square feet. One acre at 43,560 square feet is about 75.6% of a full football field, or roughly 90.7% of the playing area alone (48,000 square feet without end zones). So one acre is slightly smaller than the field between the goal lines.

How do I convert square feet to acres?

Divide the total square footage by 43,560. For example, a 10,000 square foot lot is 10,000 / 43,560 = approximately 0.2296 acres. For larger areas the math stays the same: a 100,000 square foot parcel is about 2.296 acres. This calculator does the arithmetic for you and also shows the result in hectares, square meters, square yards, and square miles.

What is a quarter acre in square feet?

A quarter acre is exactly 10,890 square feet. This is one of the most common residential lot sizes in suburban neighborhoods. If the lot were perfectly square, each side would measure approximately 104.36 feet. Many zoning codes reference quarter-acre minimums for single-family residential zones.

How many acres is a typical house lot?

Lot sizes vary widely by region. In dense urban areas, lots might be 0.05 to 0.15 acres (2,178 to 6,534 sq ft). Standard suburban lots are typically 0.17 to 0.5 acres (7,405 to 21,780 sq ft). Rural residential properties can range from 1 to 10+ acres. The median lot size for new single-family homes in the US is approximately 0.19 acres, or about 8,276 square feet.
