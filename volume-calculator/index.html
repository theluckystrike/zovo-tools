Volume Formulas

Every three-dimensional shape has a specific formula for calculating its volume, the amount of space it occupies. Below are the formulas used in this calculator.

Cube

V = a³ where a is the side length.

Rectangular Prism

V = l × w × h where l, w, and h are length, width, and height.

Cylinder

V = πr²h where r is radius and h is height.

Sphere

V = (4/3)πr³ where r is radius. First derived by Archimedes.

Cone

V = (1/3)πr²h where r is base radius and h is height.

Pyramid

V = (1/3) × base area × h where base area = l × w.

Triangular Prism

V = (1/2) × b × h_t × l where b is triangle base, h_t is triangle height, l is prism length.

Ellipsoid

V = (4/3)πabc where a, b, c are the three semi-axes.

Hemisphere

V = (2/3)πr³ where r is radius.

Torus

V = 2π²Rr² where R is major radius and r is tube radius.

Capsule

V = πr²((4/3)r + h) where r is radius and h is cylinder height.

Frustum

V = (πh/3)(R² + Rr + r²) where R and r are the two radii and h is height.

How Volume Works

Volume measures the three-dimensional space enclosed by a surface. It is expressed in cubic units: cubic meters (m³), cubic centimeters (cm³), cubic inches (in³), or cubic feet (ft³). Liquid equivalents include liters, milliliters, and gallons.

For regular geometric shapes, volume is calculated using exact mathematical formulas derived from integral calculus or, historically, from Cavalieri's principle and the method of exhaustion developed by Archimedes. For irregular shapes, methods like water displacement or numerical integration are used.

This calculator computes both volume and surface area for each shape. Surface area is the total area of all outer faces and is useful for material calculations like paint coverage, wrapping, and thermal insulation sizing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate the volume of a cylinder?
Use the formula V = πr²h, where r is the radius and h is the height. For example, a cylinder with radius 5 cm and height 10 cm has a volume of 3.14159 × 25 × 10 = 785.4 cubic centimeters.
What is the formula for the volume of a sphere?
V = (4/3)πr³. For a sphere with radius 6 cm, the volume is (4/3) × 3.14159 × 216 = 904.8 cubic centimeters. This formula was first derived by Archimedes using the method of exhaustion.
How do I convert cubic meters to liters?
Multiply cubic meters by 1,000. One cubic meter equals exactly 1,000 liters. For example, 2.5 cubic meters = 2,500 liters. This calculator converts automatically between all common volume units.
What is the difference between volume and capacity?
Volume measures the 3D space an object occupies (in cubic units like m³ or ft³). Capacity measures how much a container can hold (in units like liters or gallons). Numerically, 1 liter = 1,000 cm³.
How do you calculate the volume of an irregular shape?
Use water displacement: submerge the object and measure how much the water level rises. The displaced water volume equals the object's volume. Alternatively, decompose the shape into simpler solids and sum their volumes.
Does this calculator also compute surface area?
Yes. Every calculation includes both volume and total surface area, with formulas shown step by step. Surface area is useful for material estimates like paint, wrapping, or insulation.
