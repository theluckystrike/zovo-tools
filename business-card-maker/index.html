How to Design a Professional Business Card

A business card is one of the most direct forms of professional networking. Despite the growth of digital contact sharing, physical business cards remain a standard in business settings worldwide. This tool lets you design a card using your browser without uploading data to any server.

Start by entering your contact information in the form. The live preview updates immediately as you type. Choose one of four design templates: Modern, Elegant, Bold, or Minimal. Each template arranges the text and logo differently to suit different professional styles.

Choosing the Right Template

The Modern template places your name and title on the left with a colored divider line. It works well for tech, consulting, and corporate roles. The Elegant template centers all text and uses thinner visual elements, suited for creative professionals, consultants, and legal fields. The Bold template features a colored header bar with your name in white, useful for sales and marketing. The Minimal template removes decorative elements entirely and relies on whitespace and typography.

Color and Logo Customization

Use the primary color picker to set the background or header color depending on the template. The accent color controls divider lines and subtle highlights. Upload a company logo in PNG, JPG, SVG, or WebP format. The logo appears on both the front and back of the card. All image processing happens locally using the FileReader API.

QR Code with vCard Data

When the QR code option is enabled, the back of the card displays a QR code encoded in vCard 3.0 format. Scanning this code with a smartphone camera creates a new contact entry with the name, title, company, phone, email, website, and address you entered. The QR code is generated using a client-side JavaScript implementation with no external dependencies.

Exporting and Printing

Click Print/Export PDF to open your browser print dialog. The page uses print-improved CSS with the card sized to the standard 3.5 by 2 inch dimensions. Select "Save as PDF" as the destination to create a PDF file. For physical cards, print on 80 lb cover stock or 250 gsm cardstock and use a paper trimmer to cut precisely along the edges.

A business card is a small card printed with personal information such as a name, occupation, business affiliation, addresses, and contact data. Business cards are shared during formal introductions for convenience and as a memory aid. In many cultures, the exchange of business cards follows a set protocol and is considered a social ritual. Standard dimensions vary by country but the ISO 7810 ID-1 size (85.6 by 53.98 mm, approximately 3.37 by 2.13 inches) is used internationally, while the United States standard is 3.5 by 2 inches (89 by 51 mm).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this business card maker free?

Yes, this business card maker is completely free. There is no account creation, no watermark, and no usage limits. You can design and export as many business cards as you want at no cost.

What are the standard business card dimensions?

The standard business card size in the United States and Canada is 3.5 by 2 inches (89 by 51 mm). This tool uses these exact dimensions for the card preview and PDF export so your design will match the trim size used by most print shops.

Can I add a QR code to my business card?

Yes. The tool generates a QR code in vCard format using your contact information. When someone scans the QR code with their phone, it automatically creates a new contact entry with your name, title, company, phone, email, website, and address.

How do I export my business card as a PDF?

Click the Print/Export PDF button. Your browser print dialog will open with a clean layout improved for printing. Select Save as PDF as the destination. The output uses print-improved CSS with correct dimensions and bleed margins.

Can I upload my company logo?

Yes. Use the logo upload field in the form. The tool accepts PNG, JPG, SVG, and WebP formats. The logo is displayed on the card preview and included in the PDF export. All processing happens locally in your browser.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No data is sent to any server. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your name, phone number, email, and other contact details never leave your device. There are no cookies, no analytics, and no tracking scripts on this page.

What design templates are available?

There are four templates: Modern features a clean left-aligned layout with accent color dividers. Elegant uses centered text with thin borders and serif-inspired spacing. Bold uses a full-color header section with large name display. Minimal strips the design to essential text only with generous whitespace.

Can I print business cards at home?

Yes. Export the card as a PDF using the Print/Export PDF button. Print on cardstock (80 lb cover or 250 gsm) for best results. Most inkjet and laser printers handle cardstock well. For professional results, use a paper trimmer to cut along the card edges.
