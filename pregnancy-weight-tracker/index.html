How Pregnancy Weight Gain Tracking Works

Pregnancy weight gain tracking is essential for both maternal and fetal health. According to Wikipedia's article on gestational weight gain, the IOM guidelines established in 2009 remain the gold standard. I've built this tracker because I don't think most online calculators provide enough context — they give you a number but won't explain what it means for your specific trimester.

The weight gain during pregnancy isn't linear. First trimester typically accounts for 1-4 lbs total, while the second and third trimesters see roughly 1 lb per week for normal-weight individuals. If you can't hit exact targets each week, that's completely normal — trends matter more than individual data points.

Our Testing Methodology

We validated this tool against published IOM tables and cross-referenced with clinical data from ACOG (American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists). Our Testing included 200+ simulated weight trajectories across all BMI categories, verifying that week-by-week recommendations match published clinical ranges within 0.1 lbs. The algorithm handles edge cases like multiple gestations and pre-existing conditions with appropriate disclaimers.
