    About This Tool

    This cat years to human years calculator uses the conversion formula endorsed by the American Veterinary Medical Association (AVMA) and the American Association of Feline Practitioners (AAFP). Rather than the outdated "multiply by 7" rule, it accounts for the reality that cats mature rapidly in their first two years and then age more gradually.

    The six life stages follow the International Cat Care framework, which is adopted by veterinary professionals worldwide. The health milestones listed for each stage are general guidelines meant to raise awareness. They should not replace individualized advice from your veterinarian.
