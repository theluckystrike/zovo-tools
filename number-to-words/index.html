How to use the number to words converter

Type or paste your number into the Number field and the written form appears in the In words box instantly - there is nothing to submit. Commas in pasted numbers are ignored automatically, so 1,250,000 works exactly like 1250000. When the result looks right, press Copy words to send it to your clipboard, or press Clear to start again.

Choose how the number is spelled with the Mode menu. Cardinal spells the count, such as one hundred twenty-three, and reads decimals digit by digit after the word point. Ordinal spells rank or position, such as twenty-first or one hundred twenty-third. Currency / cheque amount writes a formal money amount with the cents shown as a fraction of one hundred, and reveals a currency menu so you can pick US dollars, British pounds, euros, or Indian rupees.

Common uses for spelling out numbers

Writing numbers as words removes ambiguity and is required in several everyday situations.

Writing cheques and checks

Banks ask you to write the payment amount in words so a figure cannot be altered. Currency mode produces the exact phrasing you need, for example One Thousand Two Hundred Thirty-Four Dollars and 50/100, ready to copy onto the cheque line.

Legal documents and contracts

Agreements, deeds, and promissory notes spell out amounts and dates in words alongside the numerals to prevent disputes. Cardinal and ordinal modes give you precise, consistent wording for both quantities and clause numbering.

Invoices and accounting

Many invoice templates and official receipts require the total in words for verification. The converter turns any total into a clean amount in seconds so your paperwork matches between the figure and the written line.

Teaching and accessibility

Spelling out numbers helps children learn place value and helps screen-reader users and language learners read figures aloud correctly. Ordinal mode is handy for teaching dates and rankings such as the twenty-first.

Everything runs locally. Your numbers never leave your device. All processing happens in your browser with JavaScript, so you can convert salaries, invoice totals, or any sensitive figure without anything being uploaded or stored.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert a number into words?
Type or paste your number into the input box and the words appear instantly below. Pick a mode first: Cardinal spells the count (one hundred twenty-three), Ordinal spells the position (one hundred twenty-third), and Currency writes it as a cheque amount. Press Copy to grab the result for your document.
What is the difference between cardinal and ordinal numbers?
Cardinal numbers state how many - one, two, three, or one hundred twenty-three. Ordinal numbers state position or rank - first, second, third, or one hundred twenty-third. Use cardinal for quantities and amounts, and ordinal for dates, rankings, and sequence such as the twenty-first item.
How does the currency or cheque mode work?
Currency mode writes the whole part as a money amount and the decimal part as a fraction of one hundred, the standard format for writing cheques. For example 1234.5 in US dollars becomes One Thousand Two Hundred Thirty-Four Dollars and 50/100. You can switch between US dollars, British pounds, euros, and Indian rupees.
How large a number can it convert?
The converter handles whole numbers up to the trillions, covering thousand, million, billion, and trillion groupings. Because very large values lose precision as plain JavaScript numbers, integers beyond about fifteen digits are read directly from the digits you type so the words stay accurate.
How are decimals and negative numbers handled?
In cardinal mode a decimal is read digit by digit after the word point, so 1234.5 becomes one thousand two hundred thirty-four point five. In currency mode the decimals become cents or paise as a fraction of one hundred. Negative values are prefixed with the word negative, and zero is simply written as zero.
Is my data private when I use this tool?
Yes. The entire conversion runs locally in your browser with JavaScript. Nothing you type is uploaded, logged, or sent to any server, so you can convert invoice totals, salaries, or sensitive figures with confidence. The tool also keeps working offline once the page has loaded.
