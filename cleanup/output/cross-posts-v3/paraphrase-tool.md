Sometimes you know what you want to say but the wording isn't working. Maybe it's too wordy, too stiff, or you just need a fresh angle on the same idea. I built a paraphrasing tool that helps with exactly that — and it works entirely in your browser.

## What It Does

Paste your text and the tool suggests alternative phrasings. It restructures sentences, swaps synonyms, and adjusts tone while keeping the original meaning intact. You can choose between different modes — simpler, more formal, shorter, or just different.

It's useful for cleaning up rough drafts, avoiding repetitive phrasing in longer pieces, or rewording something when you're stuck on how to express an idea.

## Your Text Stays Private

Here's the thing about most paraphrasing tools: they send your text to a server. That's fine for a random blog post, but what about a confidential business proposal? A personal essay? A legal document?

This tool processes everything client-side. No API calls, no server, no data stored anywhere. Your text exists in your browser tab and nowhere else. When you close the tab, it's gone.

## Why Client-Side?

I'm a big believer in client-side tools for anything that handles user content. The technology is there — JavaScript is more than capable of text manipulation, synonym lookup from a local dictionary, and sentence restructuring without needing a round trip to a server.

Is it as powerful as a server-side AI model? Honestly, no. But it's fast, private, and handles the common cases well. Sometimes good enough plus private beats amazing minus your data on someone's server.
