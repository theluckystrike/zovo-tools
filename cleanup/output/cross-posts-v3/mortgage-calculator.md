When I was shopping for a home, every mortgage calculator I found online was actually a lead capture form in disguise. Enter your info, hit calculate, and surprise — "An agent will contact you shortly." No thanks.

So I built a mortgage calculator that actually just calculates your mortgage. No signup, no data collection, no server.

## What It Does

Plug in the home price, down payment, interest rate, and loan term. You get your monthly payment instantly — broken down into principal and interest. It also shows the full amortization schedule so you can see exactly how each payment splits between paying down the loan and paying interest.

You can add property tax, homeowner's insurance, and PMI to get the real monthly cost, not just the loan payment. Because the loan payment is never the whole story.

## Privacy-First Design

Your financial details are sensitive. The home price you're considering, the down payment you can afford — that's personal information. This calculator processes everything locally in your browser. There's no server receiving your data. No cookies. No analytics tracking what numbers you enter.

View the source code if you want to verify. It's all right there in the page.

## The Amortization Breakdown

The most useful part is probably the amortization table. It shows month by month how your balance decreases, how much goes to interest vs principal, and your cumulative interest paid. Early in a 30-year mortgage, most of your payment goes to interest. Seeing that in a table makes it real in a way that just knowing the concept doesn't.
