How Unit Price Comparison Works

  • Unit Price Formula: Unit Price = Total Price / Quantity. For example, a $5.99 bottle of 64 oz juice costs $0.094 per oz
  • Why Bigger Isn't Always Better: While bulk packages often have lower unit prices, this isn't always the case. Sales, coupons, and store brands can make smaller packages cheaper per unit
  • Unit Conversion: This tool normalizes all quantities to the same unit for fair comparison. 1 lb = 16 oz, 1 kg = 35.274 oz, 1 liter = 33.814 fl oz
  • Savings Calculation: Shows exactly how much more you'd pay choosing a higher-priced option over the best value

Shopping Tips

Always check the unit price tag on store shelves - it's required by law in most US states. Compare across brands, not just sizes. Consider perishability: a larger container at a lower unit price is only a deal if you'll use it all before it expires. Store brands typically offer 20-30% lower unit prices than name brands with comparable quality.
