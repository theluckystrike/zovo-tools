What Is a Time Zone Meeting Planner?

A time zone meeting planner is a scheduling tool that helps distributed teams find overlapping working hours across different time zones. As Wikipedia notes, there are more than 24 practical time zones in use worldwide, including several at 30- and 45-minute offsets, making manual scheduling surprisingly tricky.[1]

I this tool after one too many scheduling mishaps with my own remote team. We had people in EST, CET, and IST, and honestly, I was tired of doing the mental math every time we needed a standup. So here's a visual planner that just shows you when everyone's awake and working.

It doesn't require any account, doesn't phone home, and runs entirely in your browser. The timezone data comes from the Intl API that's into modern browsers, so it's always accurate, including DST adjustments.
