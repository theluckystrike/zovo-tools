About This Tool

Built by Michael Lip, this Fibonacci sequence generator uses BigInt arithmetic for exact calculation of large Fibonacci numbers. It supports sequences up to 100 terms with full precision, golden ratio convergence analysis, and a CSS-based Fibonacci spiral visualization.

This tool was designed, coded, and tested by human developers at Zovo.one. All calculations verified against OEIS A000045 (The On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences). No generative AI was used in building this tool.
