About ASCII Art Banners
ASCII art is a graphic design technique that uses printable characters to create visual displays. According to Wikipedia's ASCII art article, ASCII art has been around since the 1960s and remains popular in programming culture. You won't find a simpler way to add visual flair to terminal output and code comments.
