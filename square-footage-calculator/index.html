How to Figure Out Square Footage

Figuring out square footage is straightforward for simple shapes. For a rectangle, multiply length by width. A 10-foot by 12-foot room is 120 square feet. For a circle, use the formula π × r², where r is the radius. For a triangle, multiply the base by the height and divide by 2.

For irregular rooms, break the space into simpler shapes. An L-shaped room can be divided into two rectangles. Measure each section separately, calculate the area of each, and add them together. This calculator handles this automatically when you select the L-shaped room option.

When measuring for flooring or painting, always measure at the widest points and round up to the nearest inch. It is better to have slightly more material than to fall short. Professional contractors typically add 10% waste factor for standard flooring and 15% for diagonal or pattern installations. For more on area measurement techniques, see the Wikipedia article on area.

Acre Square Footage Conversions

One acre equals exactly 43,560 square feet. This measurement dates back to medieval England, where an acre was defined as the area a yoke of oxen could plow in one day. Today it remains the standard land measurement in the United States, United Kingdom, and several other countries.

Square Footage Formulas Reference

Here are the formulas used by this calculator for each shape:

  • Rectangle: Area = Length × Width
  • Circle: Area = π × Radius²
  • Triangle: Area = (Base × Height) ÷ 2
  • Trapezoid: Area = ((Base1 + Base2) ÷ 2) × Height
  • L-Shaped Room: Split into two rectangles and sum: (L1 × W1) + (L2 × W2)

All formulas produce results in the square of whatever unit you input. If you enter dimensions in feet, you get square feet. If you enter meters, you get square meters. Use the unit conversion tab to translate between measurement systems. Material Estimation Guide

Estimating materials accurately prevents costly over-ordering or the frustration of running short mid-project. Here are standard waste factors used by professional contractors:

  • Paint: One gallon covers approximately 350-400 sq ft per coat. Most rooms need two coats. Textured walls require 15-20% more paint.
  • Flooring (planks): Add 10% waste for straight installation, 15% for diagonal. Plank sizes vary; a common size is 5 inches wide by 48 inches long (1.39 sq ft per plank).
  • Ceramic Tile: Add 15% waste for square layouts, 20% for diagonal. Standard tiles range from 12x12 inches (1 sq ft) to 24x24 inches (4 sq ft).
  • Carpet: Sold in 12-foot widths. Calculate based on room dimensions and add 5-10% for seaming.
  • Mulch: One cubic yard covers approximately 162 sq ft at 2 inches deep, or 108 sq ft at 3 inches deep.
  • Sod: Sold in rolls typically 2 ft by 5 ft (10 sq ft per roll). Add 5% for trimming.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I figure out square footage of a room?▼
To figure out square footage of a rectangular room, measure the length and width in feet, then multiply them together. For example, a room that is 12 feet long and 10 feet wide is 120 square feet. For irregular shapes, break the room into rectangles, calculate each area, and add them together. This calculator handles rectangles, circles, triangles, trapezoids, and L-shaped rooms automatically.
How many square feet are in an acre?▼
One acre contains exactly 43,560 square feet. An acre is roughly 208.71 feet on each side if it were a perfect square, or about 90% of a football field. To convert acres to square feet, multiply the number of acres by 43,560. To convert square feet to acres, divide by 43,560.
How do I calculate square footage for flooring?▼
Measure each room's length and width in feet and multiply to get square footage. Add up all rooms to get the total. Then add 10% for waste (cuts and mistakes). For tile, add 15% waste. This calculator includes a material estimator that handles waste calculations for flooring, tile, paint, and other materials automatically.
How do I convert square feet to square meters?▼
Multiply square feet by 0.0929 to get square meters. For example, 1,000 sq ft = 92.9 sq meters. To convert square meters back to square feet, multiply by 10.764. This calculator provides instant conversions between square feet, square meters, square yards, acres, and hectares.
