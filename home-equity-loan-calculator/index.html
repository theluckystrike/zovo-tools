Frequently Asked Questions

What is a home equity loan?
A home equity loan is a fixed-rate loan that lets you borrow against the equity in your home. You receive a lump sum upfront and repay it in equal monthly installments over a set term. It is sometimes called a second mortgage because it is secured by your home in addition to your primary mortgage.
How much can I borrow with a home equity loan?
Most lenders allow you to borrow up to 80-85% of your home value minus your existing mortgage balance. For example, with a $450,000 home and $280,000 mortgage, you could potentially borrow up to $102,500 (at 85% combined LTV). Credit score, income, and debt-to-income ratio also factor into approval.
Is a home equity loan or HELOC better?
It depends on your needs. A home equity loan provides a fixed rate and predictable payments, making it ideal for one-time expenses like home renovations. A HELOC offers flexible borrowing during the draw period, which works well for ongoing expenses. Home equity loans are better for budgeting certainty, while HELOCs offer more flexibility.
