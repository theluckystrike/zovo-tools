How to Use Emoji Favicons

Favicons are small icons displayed in browser tabs and bookmarks. Using emojis as favicons is a quick way to give your site a recognizable identity without hiring a designer. According to Wikipedia's favicon article, favicons have been supported since Internet Explorer 5 in 1999. You can't go wrong with an emoji favicon - it's a technique many developers don't know about.
