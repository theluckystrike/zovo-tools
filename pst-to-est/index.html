Understanding PST and EST

Pacific Standard Time (PST) is the time observed by the west coast of North America during the standard-time months, which run from early November through mid-March. Its offset from Coordinated Universal Time is UTC-8. Eastern Standard Time (EST) is used on the east coast during the same period with an offset of UTC-5. The difference between the two is always exactly 3 hours: Eastern is ahead.

When daylight saving time takes effect, the Pacific zone shifts to PDT (UTC-7) and the Eastern zone shifts to EDT (UTC-4). Because both zones spring forward and fall back on the same dates and at the same local time, the 3-hour gap never changes. You can always add 3 hours to go from Pacific to Eastern, regardless of the time of year.

Major cities in the Pacific time zone

Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle, Portland, San Diego, Las Vegas, and Vancouver (Canada). These cities all observe PST in winter and PDT in summer.

Major cities in the Eastern time zone

New York City, Washington D.C., Miami, Boston, Atlanta, Philadelphia, Toronto, and Montreal. All of these observe EST in winter and EDT in summer.

The 3-hour rule

A simple way to remember the conversion: if a meeting starts at 10:00 AM Pacific, it starts at 1:00 PM Eastern. Just add 3. Going the other direction, subtract 3. This holds true 365 days a year because both coasts change their clocks in lockstep.

Daylight Saving Time Details

In the United States and Canada, daylight saving time begins on the second Sunday of March at 2:00 AM local time, when clocks spring forward to 3:00 AM. It ends on the first Sunday of November at 2:00 AM, when clocks fall back to 1:00 AM.

During DST (roughly March through early November), Pacific time is referred to as PDT (Pacific Daylight Time, UTC-7) and Eastern time as EDT (Eastern Daylight Time, UTC-4). Outside DST (roughly November through mid-March), the labels revert to PST (UTC-8) and EST (UTC-5).

Key DST dates for 2026

Spring forward: Sunday, March 8, 2026 at 2:00 AM. Fall back: Sunday, November 1, 2026 at 2:00 AM. This converter detects the correct variant automatically using your browser's Intl.DateTimeFormat API with the IANA time zone identifiers America/Los_Angeles and America/New_York.

States that do not observe DST

Arizona (except the Navajo Nation) and Hawaii do not observe daylight saving time. If you are converting to or from those areas, their offset relative to PST and EST will shift by one hour during the DST transition periods. This tool is specifically for the standard Pacific and Eastern zones that do observe DST.
